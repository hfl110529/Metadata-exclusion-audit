## Interpretation and reproduction scope

The maximal-decisiveness and double-omission results are conditional mathematical claims about rectangular evidence and independent scalar completion; their assumptions and proofs are in Sections III–IV. The recall-to-cutoff bound and its sharpness construction are in Section V-F. Finite experiment counts do not establish these assumptions for every retrieval service.

Native-response checks reconstruct the upstream, backend, reader and access results; SciFact reconstructs scores from saved vectors, while ANN uses saved complete native scores. Attribution, precision, transition and rank-margin calculations start from the distributed comparison records. Migration checks validate aggregates. The private EHR study is excluded from public reproduction. Geometry verifies public certificates before consulting its separate nonclinical score oracle.

The 10,684-byte target-score projection is an output after acquisition, distinct from 121,694 acquired bytes. SQL volumes count compact JSON values; Qdrant volumes count HTTP bodies. Timings describe local measurements, and the score-log median of 10.258 ms excludes score acquisition. Replacing a log and its checkpoints can pass internal integrity checks; source comparison still rejects the forged scores.
